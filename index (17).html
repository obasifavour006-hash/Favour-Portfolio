    About me

    I started by watching what makes people stop scrolling.

    My career began in social media management. Day after day I studied platforms, algorithms, hooks and the way people behave online, and I noticed something: beautiful content doesn't guarantee results. Content has to connect with a real person.

    So I went deeper into strategy, working with brands in book publishing, real estate, e-commerce, EdTech, fashion and SaaS. Product, service or software, the lesson stayed the same.

    Whatever you sell will eventually be bought by a human. Make your marketing feel human.

    Today I combine that marketing sense with AI video to help businesses show their value in ways people relate to and remember.
